About the FitAI Platform

An end-to-end intelligent fitness ecosystem combining physiological Machine Learning regression, Multimodal Vision AI for food nutrition estimation, and relational MySQL database infrastructure.

Machine Learning Model

The calorie prediction engine is built on an XGBoost Regressor trained on 15,000 physiological exercise samples with an MAE under 1.5 kcal. It takes 7 primary biometric variables:

  • Biometrics: Age, Gender, Height (cm), Weight (kg)
  • Dynamics: Duration (min), Heart Rate (bpm), Body Temperature (°C)
  • Validation: Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of ~1.48 kcal.

Multimodal Computer Vision

The nutrition estimation engine utilizes the Experiential Labs Vision API. When a user captures or uploads a meal image:

  • Identifies culinary items and visible components.
  • Estimates volume, portion size, and caloric density.
  • Extracts macronutrients: Protein, Carbohydrates, Fat, and Fiber.
