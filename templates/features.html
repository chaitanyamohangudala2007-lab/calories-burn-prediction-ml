Platform Capabilities

Designed to cover complete fitness tracking workflows from individual workouts and nutritional intake to multi-client coaching.

Physiological Calorie Burn Prediction

XGBoost Regressor Model

Predicts total calories burned by taking into account cardiovascular response (heart rate) and thermal output (body temperature) alongside duration and physical biometrics.

Key Highlights:
  • High precision with an MAE under 1.5 kcal
  • Adapts to low, moderate, and extreme cardiovascular intensities
  • Stores historical data directly into MySQL for trend analysis

Multimodal Food Nutrition Recognition

Experiential Labs Multimodal Vision AI

Eliminates manual food diary logging. Point your camera or upload a picture of any meal to obtain estimated portion size, caloric density, and macronutrient profile.

Key Highlights:
  • Supports camera capture and image upload
  • Provides protein, carbohydrate, fat, and fiber metrics
  • Maintains long-term dietary logs with visual snapshots

Gym Trainer & Client Management

Dedicated Coach Workspace

Gym coaches can register multiple clients, log specialized training sessions, and view granular progress charts with complete data isolation.

Key Highlights:
  • Strict role-based isolation between trainers
  • Automated BMI computation and categorization
  • Detailed client drill-downs with individual Chart.js graphs

Energy Balance & Progress Tracking

Interactive Visualizations

Interactive dashboards automatically calculate caloric deficit or surplus by comparing calories burned during workouts against food calories consumed.

Key Highlights:
  • 7-day rolling calories burned vs consumed charts
  • Timeframe filters: Daily, Weekly, and Monthly
  • Macronutrient distribution doughnut visualization
